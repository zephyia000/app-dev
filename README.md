# Rapunzel (Tangled)

My favorite movie is **Rapunzel**, also known as *Tangled*. It was made by Disney and came out in 2010.

## What it's about

Rapunzel is a girl with really long magic hair who has lived in a tower her whole life. One day she leaves the tower with the help of a thief named Flynn Rider, and she finally gets to see the world and the floating lanterns.

## Characters

- Rapunzel
- Flynn Rider
- Mother Gothel
- Pascal
- Maximus

## Why I like it

1. The animation looks really nice
2. The characters are funny
3. It's about following your dreams

> My favorite part is when Rapunzel finally sees the lanterns up close.
